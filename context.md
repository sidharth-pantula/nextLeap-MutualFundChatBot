# Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant (Facts-Only Q&A) - Project Context

## 1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to build a compliant, facts-only FAQ assistant for mutual fund schemes using **Groww** as the product context and data source. The system is designed to answer objective, verifiable mutual fund queries by retrieving information strictly from the 5 designated Groww scheme pages.

> **Core Philosophy:** Prioritize **accuracy over intelligence**. Deliver verified, source-backed financial information with zero advisory bias or speculative content.

---

## 2. Core Objectives
- Design and implement a lightweight **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** assistant.
- Provide factual, verifiable answers about mutual fund schemes using a curated corpus of official documents.
- Strictly avoid investment advice, personal opinions, subjective comparisons, or recommendations.
- Ensure all responses follow a strict response format with exact citations and timestamps.

---

## 3. Target Users
- **Retail Investors:** Comparing mutual fund scheme facts (expense ratios, exit loads, lock-in periods, etc.).
- **Customer Support & Content Teams:** Handling repetitive factual queries about mutual funds and processes (statements, tax documents).

---

## 4. Scope of Work

### 4.1. Corpus Definition
The assistant's knowledge base and retrieval corpus will be strictly limited to the following **5 scheme URLs from Groww**:

1. **HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund (Direct - Growth)**
   - *URL:* `https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-mid-cap-fund-direct-growth`
   - *Category:* Mid Cap Fund
2. **HDFC Small Cap Fund (Direct - Growth)**
   - *URL:* `https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund-direct-growth`
   - *Category:* Small Cap Fund
3. **HDFC Nifty 50 Index Fund (Direct - Growth)**
   - *URL:* `https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-nifty-50-index-fund-direct-growth`
   - *Category:* Index Fund (Large Cap Benchmark)
4. **HDFC Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (Direct - Growth)**
   - *URL:* `https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-nifty-next-50-index-fund-direct-growth`
   - *Category:* Index Fund (Next 50 Benchmark)
5. **HDFC Multi Cap Fund (Direct - Growth)**
   - *URL:* `https://groww.in/mutual-funds/hdfc-multi-cap-fund-direct-growth`
   - *Category:* Multi Cap Fund

> **Note on Citations & Sources:** Every factual answer generated by the assistant will cite the exact corresponding Groww scheme URL from the above list of 5 sources.

### 4.2. FAQ Assistant Requirements & Capabilities
- **Factual Query Categories:**
  - Expense ratio (Direct vs Regular)
  - Exit load details and holding criteria
  - Minimum SIP and Lump sum investment amounts
  - ELSS lock-in periods and tax deduction rules
  - Riskometer classification
  - Benchmark index
  - Operational processes (e.g., how to download account statements or capital gains reports)
- **Response Format Contract:**
  - **Sentence Limit:** Maximum of **3 sentences** per response.
  - **Citations:** Exactly **one (1)** official source link per response.
  - **Footer:** Mandatory footer timestamp format:
    ```text
    Last updated from sources: <date>
    ```

### 4.3. Refusal & Guardrails Handling
- **Query Types to Refuse:**
  - Subjective advice / recommendations (e.g., *"Should I invest in this fund?"*, *"Is fund X good for 5 years?"*)
  - Comparative opinions (e.g., *"Which fund is better: A or B?"*)
  - Speculative returns / forward-looking projections
- **Refusal Response Policy:**
  - Be polite, objective, and clearly state the facts-only constraint.
  - Provide a relevant educational link (e.g., AMFI or SEBI investor education resources).

### 4.4. Minimal User Interface
- Welcome message introducing the assistant.
- Pre-populated example questions (at least 3 sample prompts).
- Prominent and persistent disclaimer banner:
  > **“Facts-only. No investment advice.”**

---

## 5. Constraints & Compliance

### 5.1. Data & Sourcing
- **Allowed:** Strictly the 5 specified Groww scheme URLs.
- **Prohibited:** Any unapproved third-party sources, unverified forum discussions, or speculation.

### 5.2. Privacy & Security (Zero PII)
- Strictly **DO NOT** collect, store, log, or process any Personally Identifiable Information (PII):
  - PAN or Aadhaar numbers
  - Bank / Folio account numbers
  - OTPs or passwords
  - Personal emails or phone numbers

### 5.3. Content & Advisory Restrictions
- **No investment advice:** Zero recommendations or rating judgements.
- **No return calculations / comparisons:** For any performance-related queries, direct users only to the official factsheet link without computing custom returns or historical ranking.

---

## 6. Expected Deliverables & Success Criteria

### Deliverables
1. **Source Code & RAG Pipeline:** Working implementation with retrieval and generation modules.
2. **README Document:**
   - Setup & installation instructions.
   - Selected AMC and list of chosen schemes.
   - Architecture overview (RAG pipeline, chunking, embedding, retrieval, LLM prompt templates).
   - Known limitations and assumptions.
3. **Disclaimer Snippet:** Clear presentation of the mandatory disclaimer (`“Facts-only. No investment advice.”`).

### Success Criteria
- **High Retrieval Accuracy:** Precise and grounded factual retrieval from official sources.
- **Strict Compliance:** Zero hallucination or ungrounded advisory output.
- **Formatting Adherence:** ≤ 3 sentences, 1 citation link, mandatory footer date.
- **Robust Refusals:** Graceful deflection of subjective/advisory questions to official educational resources.
- **Clean UI Experience:** Minimal, intuitive, and responsive interface.
